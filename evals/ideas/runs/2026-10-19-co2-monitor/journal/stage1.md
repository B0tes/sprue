- Built-in rules vs this product: 12 of the keypad's 17 built-in checks were skipped for lack of params (fine). `print-walls` and `print-skins` need `pcbWidthMm` and `pcbHeightMm` (the case size is computed from a PCB), so a case not built round a PCB cannot use them; naming my wall `caseWallMm` would have made `print-walls` fail as "some but not all". I wrote my own `print-walls` in rules.ts (replacing the built-in by id) and had to copy the FDM limits (1.0 mm Craftcloud, 2.0 mm JLC3DP up to 100 mm, 1.5 mm round fasteners) into design.json params, because rules.ts cannot import the skill's `PRINT_WALLS` table. The built-in `usb-power` counts only the controller's current (`controllerCurrentMa x 2 <= usbBudgetMa`); I replaced it with one that counts the sensor's 205 mA pulse, the OLED and the LED.
- My first `print-walls` rule caught a real fault: the OLED bosses (4.5 mm round a 1.7 mm pilot) left a 1.4 mm wall, under JLC3DP's 1.5 mm for fasteners; made them 5.0 mm.
